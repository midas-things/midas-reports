How this list was built

Every item is matched on the manufacturer's SKU (or the manufacturer variant that sits under it), not on product names — two stores calling the same machine by different titles will not produce a false match, and a genuine one will not slip through because of wording. Variants of one product are listed separately only where the competitor holds a specific colour or configuration.

What happens next

This is a one-off sample. The service is the same scan run for your catalogue every morning: a short list of what you are out of that a competitor can ship, ranked by the dollars attached, before your customers find out by leaving. No dashboard to log into, no integration, delivered as a file like this one.

If a weekly or daily list is useful, reply to the message that brought you here and tell me which day of the week and how many SKUs you carry — the first month is priced per report, not per seat.

Prices shown are your own listed prices at scan time, not competitor prices. "In stock" reflects the competitor's product page at scan time; a live inventory feed is not available from any of them, so an item can sell between the scan and your read.
